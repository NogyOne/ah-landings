# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local people looking for a jewelry business, available jewelry, custom work, jewelry repairs, or watch services. They need enough reliable information to decide whether to visit in person or start a conversation by message.

## Product Purpose

Provide useful information about Halisco Joyería, build trust in its products and services, and convert local searches into an in-person visit or message.

## Positioning

Halisco Joyería combines physical display-case sales of factory-made jewelry with evaluated custom jewelry work, jewelry repair, and watch services in one local business.

## Operating Context

The existing implementation is an Astro static website. Current design work is being developed as desktop and mobile mockups before implementation. Jewelry availability changes and is confirmed through an in-person visit or message rather than maintained as an online inventory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Physical display-case sale of factory-made jewelry.
- Custom jewelry work based on a house design, a previously made piece, a variation, or an external reference, subject to evaluation of material, measurements, feasibility, equipment, and capability.
- Jewelry repair services.
- Watch services.
- Conversion through WhatsApp or an in-person visit.
- This is not e-commerce. Do not promise an online catalog, current stock, prices, cart, checkout, or real-time availability.
- City, address, hours, phone number, WhatsApp number, and exact operational policies remain open facts and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

The brand name is Halisco Joyería. Use the real supplied logo assets and a refined, trustworthy, direct voice. Do not fabricate luxury claims or operational guarantees.

## Product Principles

- Help visitors understand what the business sells and what work it can evaluate before asking them to act.
- Build trust with specific, factual language and real supplied assets.
- Treat visits and messages as the conversion path, never an online checkout.
- Represent changing jewelry availability without implying a maintained catalog or live stock.
- Keep custom work and service promises bounded by physical evaluation and operational feasibility.
